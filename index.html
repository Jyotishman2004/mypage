About me

I'm a B.Tech student at North-Eastern Hill University (NEHU), currently building my career in web design and development through freelance work.

I started freelancing because I noticed that many local businesses struggle with their online presence — either they don't have a website, or the one they have doesn't actually help them. I wanted to change that by offering professional, affordable web solutions that are built around real business needs.

My approach is straightforward: I take the time to understand your business before I start designing. Every layout decision, every feature, every line of code should serve a purpose. I don't add things just because they look nice — I add them because they help you reach your goals.
